## Folders and savind
Downloaded the complete 'Td_updates#' folder when starting a project.
Keep the .toe and the toxes folder together.

Put both in one project folder with a name of your choice. This makes transferring between computers later easy. 

Once in place:
- .toe project file - **rename however you like**
- .tox files and their READMEs inside 'toxes' folder - **do not rename**

Note: Touchdesigner does not autosave. Use Cmd+S / Ctrl+S often to create backups. Touchdesigner organises them automatically for you.
#### Why?
.tox files are updated over time and if they stay inside the folder next to your project, they are replaced easily with updates. Download the updated toxes seperately when necessary and replace them in the 'toxes' folder inside your created project folder.
Toxes are build dynamic in the way that your settings in the components are saved and not changed when you update to a new .tox file.
